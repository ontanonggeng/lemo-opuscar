## Editorial decisions
Immediate reverse-spill hook, payoff visible at around 8–10s, deadpan assistant responses, no logo intro and no unnecessary end card. The original mug, keyboard and camera framing return at the ending. The remote vanishes after the purchase is undone, while LOOP continues operating because it is a separate appliance.

## Acting
Rae should communicate frustration through eye direction, asymmetrical brows, brief jaw movement and grounded shoulder/hand articulation—not only speech. LOOP uses a small synchronized lamp indicator. Mouth motion is tied to 12fps envelopes generated from complete Kokoro WAVs.
