# BLACKSTAR Short — The Undo Button: Approved-For-Previs Screenplay

**Duration:** 44 seconds. **Format:** 1080×1920, 24 fps, US English. **World:** Ellis's apartment. **Two characters:** Ellis and Pact. **No extra cast voices.**

## Final short script (time and action)

| Time | Action / staging | Dialogue |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.2s | Coffee tips over a keyboard. Ellis hits UNDO. Liquid pulls back into the mug in a physically coherent reverse motion. No title card. | — |
| 2.2–6.2s | Ellis watches the perfectly full mug, then Pact's quiet indicator. Facial reaction followed by an unhurried reply. | **ELLIS:** “Okay. That's useful.” **PACT:** “It undoes mistakes.” |
| 6.2–8.0s | Phone shows an embarrassing sent message addressed to Boss: LOVE YOU. Ellis raises the remote. | **ELLIS:** “Even that text to my boss?” |
| 8.0–15.0s | UNDO tapped. Phone shows MESSAGE UNSENT; a workplace card changes to APPLICATION REVOKED. Ellis freezes. The first clear payoff is fully visible before 15 seconds. | **PACT:** “Text removed. Job application removed, too.” **ELLIS:** “What?” |
| 15.0–22.5s | Ellis slaps UNDO again. Jump cut: blinds close, clock rewinds, his clothing changes to pajamas, and an alarm goes off. Show the transformation as deliberate stepping on twos. | **ELLIS:** “Undo that!” **PACT:** “Good morning. Again.” |
| 22.5–29.2s | He stares at the remote. The cup hovers on the edge. Annoyed, Ellis addresses the remote itself. | **ELLIS:** “Undo the dumbest decision I made today.” |
| 29.2–35.3s | Remote screen selects PURCHASE: UNDO REMOTE. The object disappears. Pact's indicator stays on (Pact is a separate smart-home system). | **PACT:** “Your purchase of the remote has been reversed.” |
| 35.3–40.4s | Original coffee spill reappears, flowing across the keyboard. A phone notification from BOSS reads LOVE YOU TOO. Focus on the phone, then Ellis. | — |
| 40.4–44.0s | Ellis looks at the ruined keyboard, then at the phone. No music under the pause. Hard cut immediately after his line. | **ELLIS:** “I liked the spill better.” |

### Sound and performance
Voice delivery: Ellis mildly frazzled, Pact dry and confident. No music cue that tells viewers when to laugh. Only motivate SFX from actual device and prop actions. Once the remote disappears, effects produced by the remote unwind, returning the original spill and the text to his boss. Leave a clean, readable closing reaction.

### Continuity check
Pact is a separate home assistant; removing the remote does not remove Pact. The phone's boss contact stays consistent. The same mug, keyboard and spill geometry must match in S01 and S08. Timeline change appears through the same framing rather than arbitrary dissolve. Hand must make contact with remote button. No reverse footage masquerading as new character performance.

### Production gates
Do not call the cast final until actual Episode 01 assets are loaded. Do not invent voice IDs. Avoid guessed TTS clip truncation. The first unintended consequence must be understood by 0:15. Check wide shots and close-ups of actual rendered frames at native resolution. Verify all spoken words and subtitles. No publishing.
