# BLACKSTAR — One Clean Fork

## Status
Original production treatment. This Short is intentionally unrelated to "The Undo Button" and uses a completely new cast.

## Premise
Two roommates have one bowl of leftover noodles and no clean fork. Nora solves this microscopic problem with a toaster-sized matter multiplier. Cal asks her to stop after the first successful copy. The device instead interprets the dirty kitchen as a household-wide utensil shortage, converts nearby matter into hundreds of forks, and finally reveals what it used as raw material: dinner.

## Cast
**NORA** — short, broad adult woman; calm, dry, mechanically competent. Costume silhouette: oversized quilted oven-mitt shoulder pads on a practical apron, heavy house shoes, tiny utility hair clip. Big touching white eyes, tiny pupils, heavy lids. Her body barely moves; comedy lives in eye direction, brow changes and one-beat reactions.

**CAL** — tall, narrow adult man; anxious, fast, physically expressive. Costume silhouette: huge crescent headphones around his neck, fitted T-shirt, too-short lounge pants, condiment-packet belt. Long arms, angular head, big white eyes with tiny pupils. Nervous jitter grows as forks multiply.

**FORKLING** — a non-verbal disgusting-cute creature assembled from three bent forks and one noodle strand. It chirps, blinks and drags itself with tine-legs. It never speaks.

## Device
**MATER-8 household matter multiplier.** Squat, battered toaster-like hardware with taped labels and a dented lever. Its cyan core is the **only glow in the film**. It has a tiny retro terminal display. Hard-edged metal shadows; no gradients.

## World and palette
One apartment kitchen only. Palette: beige plaster, brown cabinetry, dusty orange appliances, muted navy shadows, warm skin. Cyan belongs exclusively to MATER-8's glow. Eye white and near-purple-black outlines remain constant.

## STYLE.md compliance
- Uniform thick near-purple-black outlines, screen-space constant; 3-drawing line boil at 12fps on characters, props and background.
- Flat fills only; one hard-edged shadow per major form. No gradients.
- Characters act on twos (12fps); camera pushes, flying forks and machine effects move on ones (24fps).
- Big white eyes + tiny pupils; heavy lids for Nora; elastic mouth shapes from audio RMS.
- Dialogue-driven deadpan-vs-panic two-hander, with interruptions, cold pauses and reaction close-ups.
- Tiny mundane goal solved with absurd science.
- Device is household junk; only device glows.
- Every absurd visual is followed by a reaction close-up.
- Subtitles: Baloo 2 ExtraBold, white with thick dark outline, speaker pill, clear bottom band.
- No existing-show characters, silhouettes, names, catchphrases, logos or portal devices.

## Difference from the library demo
No portal, no universe travel, no coffee errand, no title slam, no world montage, no same rejection word, no decaf reversal, no end card. The film stays in one kitchen and escalates through prop physics, reactions, and matter-conservation consequences.

## Retention hypothesis
The first frame is already the consequence, with central conflict legible immediately. The premise is fully clear by ~13s and the first payoff is before 16s. Escalation is visual and causal rather than exposition-heavy. This is a creative hypothesis, not a guarantee of retention.
